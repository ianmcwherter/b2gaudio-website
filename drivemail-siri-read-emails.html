Can Siri read my emails to me?

Sort of — but not the way you want while driving. Here's what Siri can and can't do, and the fix.

Short answer: Siri can read a single email notification aloud if you catch it as it arrives, but it can't open your inbox, read your unread messages in order, summarize a long email, or compose and send a real reply. For genuine hands-free email in the car, you need a dedicated app.

DriveMail Voice does exactly that: it reads all your unread email aloud on CarPlay and lets you reply or forward by voice.
