10. Prices Are Estimates, Not Appraisals

This is important, so it is stated plainly:

  • "Ungraded" prices come from a third-party market data feed and reflect recent market activity for a printing, not the specific physical card you hold.
  • Graded (PSA 10 / CGC 10) figures are drawn from current asking prices in public listings — what sellers are asking, not what cards have actually sold for. Asking prices typically run well above realised sale prices.
  • Prices ignore condition, centring, authenticity, edition, and every other factor that determines what a real card is worth.
  • Values change constantly and the figures shown may be out of date.
