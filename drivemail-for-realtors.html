Email for realtors, read aloud on the road

Between showings, open houses, and closings, your car is your second office. DriveMail keeps your inbox moving while you drive.

The problem: in real estate, minutes matter — an offer, a lender reply, a client question. But you're in the car all day, and reading email at the wheel isn't safe or legal. DriveMail Voice reads your unread email aloud on CarPlay and lets you reply or forward by voice, so you respond in the moment without ever touching your phone.
